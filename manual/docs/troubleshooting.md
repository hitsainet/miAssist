# Troubleshooting

## A result says an app is unavailable

The app could not be reached when miAssist last checked. The result names the reason. Try again
later, or ask the app's operator; retrying in a loop does not help.

## A result says a request is waiting for approval

The app's operator decides it in that app. Check its state with `miassist_approval_status`.

## My settings were not remembered

Settings are remembered only through a personal link (see [Connect](./connect.md)). If the settings
store is down, miAssist keeps working at the beginner level and says your settings are not
remembered.
